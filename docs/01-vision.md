# Vision

## One paragraph

You are the newest Keeper of a small, impossible institution: a night desk whose charter is to
witness every possible ordering of a deck of cards — all **52! ≈ 8.07 × 10⁶⁷** of them — before the
lamp goes out. You do this the way keepers always have: by playing solitaire. Every card you play
home *wakes*, and an awake card keeps counting arrangements on its own, forever, even while you
sleep. Play a few hands a day and the deck quietly hums along. Lean in, and you'll find Marks that
make cards interact, Ways that reshape a run, numbering systems that redistribute the deck's value,
and — at 52! — a door to a deck that has never existed.

## Who it's for

Two players, one game:

1. **The relaxer.** Plays two or three hands of solitaire a day, on an iPad, in landscape, probably
   in bed. Wants the cards to feel like cards. Should never *need* to open a panel to enjoy the game,
   and should still see the number climb and the sky fill in.
2. **The incremental aficionado.** Wants fast numerical leveling, layers, prestige timing, builds,
   and the satisfaction of a rate going vertical. Should find real depth: Marks that combine, Ways
   that change the run, systems that make an optimal build stop being optimal.

The relaxer must never feel the aficionado's systems as pressure; the aficionado must never feel the
relaxer's pace as a wall. **Every aficionado system is optional and reveals itself only when reached.**

## Pillars

1. **The solitaire is real.** Proper rules, proper deals, winnable-deal generation where the variant
   allows it, undo, and the feel of physical cards. If the solitaire is a chore the whole thing fails.
2. **Feel first.** Selecting, dragging, dropping, tossing, shuffling, pressing — all tuned, all
   tunable. Sound and haptics are part of feel, not garnish. ASMR is a design goal.
3. **The tableau builds the machine.** No bolted-on clicker. Cards are the generators; playing them
   is how you build and upgrade the engine.
4. **Quiet depth.** Systems arrive one at a time, when earned. Nothing revealed is ever re-hidden.
   No shop, no gacha, no card collecting. Powers are placed, not pulled.
5. **A reason to be here.** The 52! journey is a real number with real milestones (sand on Earth,
   atoms in a body, stars in the sky). Previous Keepers left notes. The window fills with stars.
6. **Modular games.** A solitaire variant or matching game is one module implementing one contract.
   Klondike first; TriPeaks, Golf, Pyramid, FreeCell, Spider to follow.
7. **Respect the player's time.** Offline earns. Nothing punishes absence. Sessions of 2, 10, or
   40 minutes are all complete experiences.

## Tone

Endearing, dry, unhurried. Lore in margins, not cutscenes. The UI whispers. Celebrations are
warm, not loud — a card cascade, a lamp flicker, a new constellation in the window. Fun facts about
magnitude are delivered as ledger entries, never as pop-ups that block play.

## Not this

- Not Balatro. No run-shop, no jokers you draw, no scoring hands. Sessions are hands of solitaire.
- Not a card collector. The deck is always *your* 52 (then 53, 56…). You mark cards; you don't
  acquire them.
- Not a clicker. Tapping the deck does something small and pleasant, but the game never asks for it.
- Not neon. No razzle-dazzle. See [09-art-direction.md](09-art-direction.md).
