# Look and sound

## Look
- Night Desk (chosen). Alternatives considered: Cosmic Observatory (prior), Coffee Shop (v1 notes),
  Library, Train Journey (each milestone a station — lovely, maybe as a *theme* later).
- The lamp as the "prestige ready" signal: it brightens as a Cut becomes worthwhile; the Cut button
  is the lamp's pull-chain.
- Awake cards glow faintly from behind when face-down: you can see the deck's warmth in the stock.
- Season/time-of-day: real clock changes the window (dusk → night → pre-dawn). Never affects play.
- Ink wear: heavily charged cards get slightly softer, more "handled" edges. Tiny.

## Sound (ASMR)
- Synth first (ADR-013). Layers: paper body (band-passed noise 1.5–4 kHz), edge click (short 6–9 kHz
  tick), felt thud (low 120 Hz sine, 40 ms) mixed by velocity.
- Riffle = 52 micro clicks with jittered timing following two interleaving envelopes; the bridge is a
  descending flutter.
- Squaring the deck: three soft taps.
- The lamp: a very faint filament hum only in the settings screen (so it's discoverable, not annoying).
- Win: no fanfare; a soft chord that resolves and the cascade's landing sounds do the work.
- Haptics mirror sounds at lower resolution.

## Type & numbers
- Odometer digits roll like a mechanical counter (each digit a vertical strip, spring to position).
