# Games and minigames

## Variants (see docs/06-games.md for the roster)
Klondike, TriPeaks, Golf, Pyramid, FreeCell, Spider. Also: Yukon (Klondike without stock),
Canfield, Forty Thieves (two decks), Accordion (tiny, weird), Clock (pure luck — an idle toy).

## Toys (not scored, ASMR)
- **Idle Riffle**: hold the deck, it riffles forever; trickle of shuffles; different grips change the sound.
- **The Spread**: fan the deck across the felt with a swipe; flick cards back into the stack.
- **House of Cards**: stack face-down cards into a pyramid; tap to collapse. Pure toy.
- **Card toss into a hat**: throw cards across the felt at a target; misses slide off the table edge with a sound.

## Matching games (modular contract fits)
- Pyramid (pairs summing to 13), Golf (±1), Monte Carlo (adjacent pairs), Memory (concentration —
  face-down pairs; every match "homes" both).

## Daily
- Daily deal (shared seed). Weekly "Keeper's Challenge": a constraint (no undo / draw 3 / Wild off).
