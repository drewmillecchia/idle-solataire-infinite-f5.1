# Mechanics ideas (2026-09-05)

## Charge decay as a *gentle* pressure (rejected for now)
Charge could fade if a card isn't played for days. Rejected: punishes absence (pillar 7). Alternative:
**Warmth** — a card played today glows and earns +10 % until midnight. Reward presence, never punish absence.

## Suit specialisation ("Devotion")
Playing lots of one suit home builds Devotion in that suit → unlocks suit-flavoured Marks (Hearts:
Twin/Echo social marks; Spades: Anchor/Heavy sturdy marks; Diamonds: Lantern/Tithe wealth marks;
Clubs: Kindling/Wild growth marks). Devotion is per-run; total Devotion feeds a Constellation branch.

## The Hand (equipped upgrades), from v1 workshop notes
Five slots of run-upgrades you carry into a hand. We folded this into Marks (slots on cards). Keep
the idea of *pre-hand planning*: a "square up" moment before dealing where you can re-place Marks.

## Streaks
Winning consecutive hands raises burst ×1.1 per win up to ×2. Losing resets softly (×0.8, not to 1).

## Quantum cards / entangled pairs (v1 notes) → became **Twin**.

## Broken cards / penalties (v1 notes)
A Way of the Gambler flavour: one card per hand is "cracked" and pays nothing but if it comes home
last it pays ×10. Optional; adds tension for variance lovers.

## Dark cards
Face-down cards that are *never* revealed until played home — a Scholar/Gambler cross. Probably too mean.

## Card base value tables (v1 notes) → Numbering Systems. Also "Lucky numbers" and "Cubes" as
Permutation-cheap novelty systems.

## Charge overflow → Tempering
At charge 50 a card can be **tempered**: charge resets to 0, permanent output ×3, and a visible
brass edge. Repeatable; each tempering costs more charge. Gives a long per-card ladder the relaxer
achieves by just playing.

## Momentum
Rate of *change* of deckRate over the last 60 s shown as a small needle. Purely informational, very idle-game.

## Auto-Dealer personalities
Cautious (never redeals), Hasty (draws fast, wins less), Careful (lookahead). Unlock on the Dealer
branch; watching them play differently is fun.

## Bets
Before a hand, wager 10 % of balance on winning; pays ×3. Gambler only.

## Daily deal
One seed per day shared by everyone (deterministic from date). Same hand for all players; a leaderboard
that only needs moves-count and time, which is verifiable client-side-ish. Cheap community feature.
